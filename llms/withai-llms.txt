# WithAI

> WithAI is a San Francisco-based Y Combinator (Spring 2026) company building Multiplier, a secure AI platform for institutional investors and independent equity hedge funds. Multiplier unifies structured data, unstructured data, AI agents, human work, and firm knowledge so investment teams can research stocks and manage portfolios more efficiently.
